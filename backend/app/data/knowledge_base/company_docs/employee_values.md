# RecruitIQ Employee Values (Behavioral Expectations)

## Purpose
Provide an internal values document that is usable during recruiting and performance review. It describes how values translate into measurable behaviors and how RecruitIQ AI should generate values-aligned question suggestions.

## Scope
Applies to:

- Recruiters and interview panels
- Managers conducting performance reviews and coaching

## Main Content
### 1. Values translated into observable behaviors
RecruitIQ values are operationalized so interviewers can score evidence:

- Respect and professionalism
  - candidates communicate clearly and professionally
  - they show empathy and appropriate context
- Ownership and follow-through
  - they deliver outcomes, not just tasks
  - they close loops and document learnings
- Continuous learning
  - they adapt, ask for feedback, and iterate
- Integrity in decision-making
  - they explain trade-offs and limitations
  - they do not overclaim results
- Customer and stakeholder focus
  - they clarify impact and success metrics

### 2. Behavioral evidence examples
Interviewers should look for:

- what the candidate did personally
- how they collaborated and communicated
- outcomes and metrics
- how they handled ambiguity

### 3. How AI supports values evaluation
RecruitIQ AI may:

- suggest behavioral questions aligned to values
- summarize draft notes from structured inputs

Rules for responsible AI usage:

- AI outputs are decision-support only
- human reviewers must validate accuracy
- low confidence in the evidence requires deeper probing

### 4. Values alignment during hiring
Interview planning uses:

- competency model for values-relevant behaviors
- structured scoring rubrics

Decision rule:

- values-based decisions should be supported by multiple evidence points across the interview loop.

### 5. Values alignment during performance
During performance reviews:

- managers reference documented examples
- employees receive clear coaching plans for improvement

## Best Practices
- Ask for concrete stories rather than abstract statements.
- Avoid scoring on charisma alone.

## Examples
### Example: Integrity
- Candidate explains a trade-off where they chose the safer approach and validated outcomes.

## Summary
RecruitIQ employee values are made measurable via observable behaviors. Recruiters and managers use structured, evidence-based interviews and reviews, with RecruitIQ AI assisting question preparation and note summarization under human validation.
