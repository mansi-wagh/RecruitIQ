# RecruitIQ Recruiter Guide: Hiring Best Practices (AI-Augmented)

## Purpose
Provide practical best practices for running reliable, candidate-friendly, evidence-based hiring processes with AI decision support. The guidance focuses on transparency, calibration, and human accountability.

## Scope
Applies to:

- Full-cycle recruiting operations
- Recruiters and hiring managers
- Interview panels using RecruitIQ AI recommendations

## Main Content
### 1. Start with a competency model
Best practice:

- align job description with a competency model
- define must-have competencies and expected proficiency levels

Why it matters for AI:

- AI match scores are only as useful as the competency model.

### 2. Use structured processes to reduce variability
Recruiting reliability improves when teams standardize:

- interview loops
- question selection
- scoring rubrics
- documentation formats

### 3. Keep AI as decision-support
RecruitIQ’s AI supports:

- resume parsing and skill gap identification
- interview question suggestions
- drafting summaries of decision rationales

Humans must:

- validate evidence
- confirm rubric mapping
- document overrides

### 4. Implement calibration sessions
Recommended calibration cadence:

- before scaling to new teams or roles
- when interviewers are new
- when rubric drift is suspected

Calibration activities:

- compare sample interview notes
- align rating anchors (“Strong/Meets/Developing”)
- update question interpretations

### 5. Ensure AI explainability
During use of AI output:

- request evidence-based explanations within the platform
- verify retrieved context is relevant to the role competency

Operational rule:

- if the AI explanation cannot be tied to candidate evidence, treat it as unverified.

### 6. Bias mitigation practices
AI and humans can both introduce bias. Reduce risk by:

- using structured questions
- scoring evidence consistently
- avoiding over-weighting of resume formatting or keyword density

### 7. Candidate experience standards
Maintain a predictable candidate journey:

- clear status updates
- respectful interview conduct
- transparent communication about next steps

### 8. Red flags and escalations
Escalate when:

- panel evidence conflicts with AI recommendation
- multiple interviewers rate competencies inconsistently
- the candidate story appears incomplete or contradictory

Use additional assessment rather than defaulting to AI conclusions.

## Best Practices
- Document “why” decisions in the recruiter note.
- Use AI to find blind spots, not to replace reasoning.

## Examples
### Example: Calibration prevents scoring drift
- Two interviewers score the same competency differently.
- Calibration shows rubric interpretation mismatch.
- Updated anchors improve scoring consistency.

## Summary
RecruitIQ hiring best practices combine competency models, structured interviews, calibration, and responsible AI decision-support usage. Human evidence validation and documented overrides are central to reliability and fairness.
