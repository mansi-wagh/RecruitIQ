# RecruitIQ DevOps Interview Guide

## Purpose
Provide a practical, competency-driven DevOps interview guide for evaluating operational thinking, reliability engineering, automation, and incident management.

## Scope
Applies to:

- DevOps/SRE/platform engineering roles
- technical panels assessing systems reliability and operational maturity

## Main Content
### 1. Competencies
Common DevOps competencies:

- CI/CD and automation
- infrastructure-as-code principles
- monitoring, alerting, and incident response
- containerization fundamentals
- security-by-design and access control

### 2. Interview structure
Suggested flow:

- operational scenario: troubleshoot a failure
- delivery pipeline: discuss CI/CD improvements
- reliability design: SLOs, retries, backoff
- security posture: secrets handling and least privilege

### 3. AI-assisted question selection
RecruitIQ may generate question candidates based on skill gaps.

Allowed:

- use as a starting list
- select follow-ups matching rubric competencies

Required:

- verify candidates demonstrate practical operational reasoning

### 4. Question frameworks
- incident response:
  - “Walk through how you would triage a latency spike.”
- automation:
  - “Describe a pipeline improvement. What was measured before and after?”
- infrastructure:
  - “How do you manage configuration drift and environment parity?”
- observability:
  - “Which metrics and logs would you inspect and why?”
- security:
  - “Explain how you handle secrets and permissions in deployments.”

### 5. Scoring guidance
Strong evidence includes:

- clear troubleshooting steps and decision criteria
- understanding trade-offs (cost vs reliability)
- evidence that automation reduced toil
- correct security and access control thinking

### 6. Human override triggers
Override AI inferences when:

- candidate demonstrates operational depth not captured in resume
- AI suggests a question unrelated to the real competence gap

## Best Practices
- Prefer scenario-based questions.
- Require specific examples and measurable impact.

## Examples
### Example: latency incident
Follow-up probes:

- “How would you confirm whether it’s DB vs network vs cache?”
- “How would you prevent recurrence?”

## Summary
This DevOps guide emphasizes scenario-based evidence and operational reasoning. RecruitIQ AI supports preparation while panels maintain rubric-based, evidence-first scoring.
