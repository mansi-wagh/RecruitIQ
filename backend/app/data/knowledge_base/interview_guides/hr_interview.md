# RecruitIQ HR (Recruiter) Interview Guide

## Purpose
Standardize the HR/recruiter interview component to validate role alignment, communication clarity, motivation, and practical fit. This guide also defines how RecruitIQ AI outputs must be interpreted as decision-support.

## Scope
Applies to:

- Recruiter screens and HR interviews
- Candidates across technical and non-technical functions

Jurisdiction-agnostic.

## Main Content
### 1. Objectives of the HR interview
The HR interview should determine:

- candidate motivation and role clarity
- communication quality and responsiveness
- logistics feasibility (availability, location, remote readiness)
- alignment with team values and working norms

It should not replace structured technical or behavioral panels.

### 2. Interview question framework
Use competency-informed categories. Example categories:

- motivation and goals
- collaboration and communication
- conflict handling
- learning and adaptation
- expectations alignment (scope, pace, level)

Preferred pattern:

- ask for a specific example when feasible
- probe for what the candidate learned, changed, or achieved

### 3. Using RecruitIQ AI recommendations
RecruitIQ may provide:

- match score interpretation
- skill gap summary
- resume improvement notes

Recruiter responsibilities:

- confirm candidate understanding of role expectations
- identify inconsistencies between resume claims and candidate explanations
- ensure the candidate’s communication style matches the team’s collaboration norms

Decision rule:

- if AI indicates low confidence in role fit, require additional clarifications or route to assessment

### 4. Candidate experience standards
Recruiters must maintain:

- respectful pacing
- clear explanation of the next steps
- consistent questions for comparable candidates

### 5. Logistics and compensation alignment
For logistics and compensation alignment:

- discuss compensation bands and expectations transparently
- confirm constraints (time zone, remote work ability, relocation if applicable)

Avoid sensitive personal attributes unrelated to job performance.

### 6. Documentation expectations
Record:

- summary of candidate motivation
- key concerns and why they matter for the role
- whether AI-suggested gaps were validated or resolved

### 7. Human override triggers
Override AI conclusions when:

- the candidate provides missing context that changes the assessment
- the candidate demonstrates strong communication and role fit despite resume gaps

## Best Practices
- Use a consistent script but tailor follow-ups to real candidate statements.
- Treat AI outputs as hypotheses to validate.

## Examples
### Example: Communication probe
If a candidate gives generic answers, ask:

- “What exactly was your contribution?”
- “What trade-off did you decide between?”

## Summary
The HR interview guide ensures structured, evidence-based evaluation with AI used for preparation and draft interpretation only. Human decisions and documentation remain the final authority.
