# RecruitIQ Candidate FAQ: Interview Process

## Purpose
Clarify how interviews are scheduled and conducted when RecruitIQ AI supports recruiter preparation and question selection. The FAQ emphasizes transparency: AI provides decision support, while human panels evaluate candidates using structured rubrics.

## Scope
Applies to:

- Candidates invited to interviews through RecruitIQ
- Recruiters and HR coordinators supporting candidate communications

## Main Content
### 1. What interview steps should I expect?
A typical sequence:

- Recruiter or HR screen
- Technical interview(s) and/or practical assessment
- Behavioral interview with structured scoring
- Panel calibration and final decision

Some roles may include additional stages (e.g., case study, system design discussion).

### 2. How are interview questions selected?
RecruitIQ may suggest questions based on:

- missing skills identified from resume signals
- role competency expectations

Human responsibility:

- Interviewers choose which questions to use and tailor follow-ups.
- The panel uses the rubric to score evidence.

### 3. Will you reject me based on AI match score?
No. Match score and confidence signals are internal inputs. Recruiters may route you to:

- structured assessment when evidence is unclear
- deeper probing when AI confidence is medium

### 4. How is my performance evaluated?
Interviews are evaluated through:

- competency-based rubrics
- evidence-based notes (what you did, what you decided, measurable outcomes)
- panel scoring before group discussion

### 5. What if I do not have a specific skill you expected?
Interviewers will usually:

- probe for equivalent experience (transferable skills)
- ask about learning approach and problem-solving

RecruitIQ guidance is designed to validate competencies, not exclude on a single missing keyword.

### 6. How do I prepare?
Preparation tips:

- review the role’s competency areas
- prepare 2–3 examples (projects or experiences) with clear scope and outcomes
- practice explaining trade-offs and decisions

If RecruitIQ provides a skill-gap summary, use it to focus your prep.

### 7. Can I request accommodations?
If your workflow supports accommodations, contact the recruiter for coordination.

RecruitIQ strives for fair, accessible interviews.

## Best Practices
- Be explicit about your contributions.
- Provide metrics and constraints whenever possible.

## Examples
### Example: Follow-up questions
If the interviewer asks about performance testing, they may follow up:

- “What metric did you use?”
- “What was your troubleshooting strategy?”

## Summary
RecruitIQ interview processes are structured and competency-based. AI helps interviewers prepare questions and validate coverage, but humans evaluate candidates using rubrics and evidence.
