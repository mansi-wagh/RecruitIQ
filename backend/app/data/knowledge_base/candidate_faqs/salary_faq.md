# RecruitIQ Candidate FAQ: Salary and Compensation

## Purpose
Clarify how salary expectations are handled in RecruitIQ hiring workflows, including AI-assisted screening boundaries and recruiter responsibilities. This FAQ is designed to keep messaging transparent and consistent.

## Scope
Applies to:

- Candidates discussing compensation during RecruitIQ hiring

## Main Content
### 1. How do you handle salary expectations?
Recruiters typically:

- confirm your compensation preferences or target range
- compare them to the role’s internal compensation bands
- discuss feasibility and alignment with role scope and level

### 2. Does AI decide your salary fit?
No. RecruitIQ AI may help generate structured interview summaries or identify role level signals, but salary decisions require human review.

### 3. What information should I provide?
Candidates may be asked for:

- current compensation (optional)
- expected compensation range
- flexibility and constraints (e.g., timing, location)

Provide what you are comfortable sharing.

### 4. When will salary be discussed?
Common timings:

- during the recruiter screen
- after panel consensus for advanced-stage candidates

Timing can vary by role.

### 5. How do bonuses or benefits work?
If applicable, recruiters explain:

- variable components and eligibility
- benefits overview at a high level
- how compensation relates to performance and role level

### 6. Can I negotiate?
In many workflows, candidates can discuss their expectations.

- Recruiters align on a mutually agreeable range
- Any final terms must be approved by HR and hiring authorities

### 7. What if my expectations are higher than the band?
Recruiters may:

- explore whether the role level can adjust
- discuss alternate roles where scope matches your range
- evaluate evidence fit for the target role

## Best Practices
- Give a range rather than a single number when possible.
- Tie expectations to your experience and impact.

## Examples
### Example: Candidate asked for flexibility
- “My target is within the range we discussed. I’m open to discussing total compensation based on role scope and level.”

## Summary
RecruitIQ handles compensation discussions through structured recruiter conversations. AI does not autonomously determine salary outcomes; humans align expectations with role scope and internal bands.
