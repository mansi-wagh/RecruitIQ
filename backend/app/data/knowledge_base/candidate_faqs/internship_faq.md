# RecruitIQ Candidate FAQ: Internships

## Purpose
Explain how internship hiring typically works in RecruitIQ, including how AI decision-support is used for screening and question planning while keeping human-led decisions.

## Scope
Applies to:

- Candidates applying for internships
- Recruiting teams supporting internship pipelines

## Main Content
### 1. What makes internships different?
Internship programs emphasize:

- learning potential and practical execution
- ability to work with guidance
- evidence of foundational competency

The evaluation should be competency-based, not purely experience-year-based.

### 2. How do you evaluate internship candidates?
RecruitIQ may support screening by:

- parsing relevant coursework or projects
- mapping skills to internship competency expectations
- identifying likely skill gaps to probe during interviews

Decision-support boundary:

- internship offers are not automated by AI

### 3. What should I include in my internship resume?
Include:

- projects with clear learning and outcomes
- relevant coursework and technical foundation
- teamwork and collaboration examples

### 4. What interview steps can I expect?
Common components:

- HR screen
- technical assessment (may be lighter than full-time roles)
- behavioral interview focusing on learning agility and collaboration

### 5. How should I prepare?
Preparation recommendations:

- review the role description
- prepare 2–3 project stories with clear scope
- practice explaining trade-offs and debugging approaches

### 6. Will missing skills automatically disqualify me?
Not necessarily.

- missing-skill findings usually guide interview probing
- interviewers evaluate learning approach and evidence of capability

### 7. Do you use AI match scores for internship decisions?
Internally, AI signals may influence screening triage.

- recruiters must validate with evidence and structured interviews
- low confidence requires additional human review

## Best Practices
- Be transparent about what you learned vs what you fully owned.
- Show evidence of progress and iteration.

### Example: Project shows partial coverage

## Summary
RecruitIQ internship evaluations emphasize competency evidence, learning potential, and structured interview probing. AI provides decision support, while humans make final decisions based on rubric evidence and fairness checks.
