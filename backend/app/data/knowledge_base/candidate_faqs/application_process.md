# RecruitIQ Candidate FAQ: Application Process

## Purpose
Explain the end-to-end application flow for candidates applying through RecruitIQ. This FAQ is designed for retrieval and consistent messaging, and it also clarifies how AI decision-support may affect the experience without making autonomous decisions.

## Scope
Applies to:

- All candidates using RecruitIQ job applications
- RecruitIQ support teams answering common questions

## Main Content
### 1. How do I apply?
Candidates typically follow these steps:

- Find an open role in RecruitIQ
- Review the job description and requirements
- Submit a resume/CV and any optional portfolio links
- Provide the requested intake fields (e.g., skills, experience range)

### 2. What does RecruitIQ do with my information?
RecruitIQ uses AI and retrieval-based techniques to:

- parse resume content into structured signals (skills, experience themes)
- compare signals to the role competency model
- suggest interview questions or resume improvements for recruiters

Decision-support boundary:

- RecruitIQ AI recommends next steps, but humans make the final hiring decisions.

### 3. What happens after I submit?
Common timelines depend on role demand. Typical workflow:

- initial screening
- recruiter review
- interview planning
- interviews and panel scoring
- final decision and communication

### 4. Do you automatically accept or reject me?
No. AI may generate:

- a match score and confidence signal
- a skill-gap summary

Recruiters use these as inputs, and additional human review occurs when:

- the AI confidence is low or medium
- evidence is ambiguous or incomplete

### 5. How can I improve my application?
Recommended actions:

- ensure your resume includes relevant skills with evidence examples
- describe project scope and outcomes
- keep your skill list aligned with what you actually did

### 6. Can I update my application?
In many workflows, candidates can update documents prior to a cutoff.

- If updates are allowed, submit the corrected resume or additional links
- RecruitIQ will re-run parsing and update the decision-support inputs

### 7. Who sees my information?
Information may be viewed by:

- recruiters and hiring panel members for the role
- HR partners responsible for review and process steps

## Best Practices
- Use consistent titles for tools/technologies you truly used.
- Add measurable outcomes when describing projects.

## Examples
### Example: AI suggests a skill gap
- RecruitIQ may recommend additional interview probing for a missing competency.
- That does not mean rejection; it means interview preparation needs clarification.

## Summary
RecruitIQ’s application process is structured and AI-assisted. AI influences the workflow by recommending next steps, but humans make final decisions based on evidence and structured rubrics.
