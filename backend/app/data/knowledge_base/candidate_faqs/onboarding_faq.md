# RecruitIQ Candidate FAQ: Onboarding

## Purpose
Describe the onboarding process after a candidate accepts an offer through RecruitIQ. This FAQ provides clarity on what to expect, how AI may help with planning drafts, and the human ownership of final onboarding arrangements.

## Scope
Applies to:

- Candidates who accept offers
- Hiring operations and HR onboarding coordinators

## Main Content
### 1. What happens after I accept an offer?
Typically:

- HR confirms documentation requirements
- onboarding plan is prepared
- the hiring manager sets role-specific expectations
- access to systems and tools is scheduled

### 2. How does RecruitIQ support onboarding?
RecruitIQ may:

- summarize interview outcomes into a structured onboarding briefing
- draft onboarding checklists
- suggest learning resources aligned to the role competency model

Decision-support boundary:

- managers approve onboarding plans and ensure accuracy
- AI does not autonomously schedule or enforce onboarding steps

### 3. What should I prepare before day one?
Candidates commonly provide:

- required documentation
- confirmation of start date and work arrangement
- access setup requirements (as applicable)

Follow HR instructions in the onboarding message.

### 4. What will I learn in the first weeks?
A typical onboarding includes:

- team and product context
- codebase/process introductions
- security and compliance training (as applicable internally)
- a first milestone with clear acceptance criteria

### 5. How are performance expectations communicated?
Expectations are communicated through:

- role competencies
- probation or early-period milestones
- scheduled check-ins

RecruitIQ may compile notes into summaries, but managers run the evaluation.

### 6. Will I receive a feedback plan?
Yes, typically:

- recurring 1:1s
- structured progress reviews
- a development plan for gaps

AI can assist by drafting summaries based on manager inputs.

### 7. Who do I contact if I have questions?
Your HR contact and hiring manager are primary points of contact.

If you need immediate help, use the onboarding communication channel provided.

## Best Practices
- Confirm your onboarding checklist items early.
- Ask questions about success metrics for your first milestone.

## Examples
### Example: Onboarding includes a first milestone
- Outcome: after a short ramp-up, you deliver a small feature with tests and documentation.

## Summary
RecruitIQ onboarding is a structured HR + manager-led process. AI may draft onboarding plans and summaries, but human leaders approve and own decisions and performance expectations.
