# RecruitIQ Technical Guide: Kubernetes (Interview Evidence)

## Purpose
Guide interviewers in evaluating Kubernetes competency. The guide focuses on orchestration fundamentals, workload configuration, operational thinking, and reliability.

## Scope
Applies to:

- DevOps/SRE/platform roles
- Panels assessing orchestration knowledge and operational reasoning

## Main Content
### 1. Core Kubernetes competencies
Assess:

- Workload basics:
  - pods, deployments, services
- Scaling and availability:
  - replicas, rolling updates
- Configuration management:
  - config maps, secrets usage (conceptually)
- Networking basics:
  - service discovery and traffic routing
- Observability mindset:
  - logs/metrics/troubleshooting approach
- Operational safety:
  - rollback strategy and risk awareness

### 2. Evidence-seeking question patterns
- “Describe how you would roll out a new version safely. What risks do you mitigate?”
- “How do you diagnose a failing pod? What signals do you inspect?”
- “Explain how you configure environment variables and sensitive settings safely.”

### 3. Strong evidence indicators
- Candidate provides step-by-step troubleshooting.
- Candidate understands trade-offs (availability vs rollout speed).
- Candidate references monitoring and validation.

### 4. AI decision-support boundaries
RecruitIQ AI may suggest Kubernetes topics based on skill gaps.

Allowed:

- question targeting and follow-up suggestions

Not allowed:

- automatic proficiency inference based on AI summary

Override:

- if candidate shows equivalent orchestration reasoning and operational outcomes, do not reject solely for missing keywords.

## Best Practices
- Prefer scenario-based troubleshooting prompts.
- Require evidence of operational validation.

## Examples
### Example: Rolling update
- Candidate explains staged rollout, health checks, monitoring, and rollback triggers.

## Summary
This Kubernetes guide defines evidence-based evaluation criteria for orchestration fundamentals and operational reliability. RecruitIQ AI supports preparation, while interviewers validate competence with human-led scoring.
