# RecruitIQ Employee Referral Policy (Talent Flow)

## Purpose
Define how employee referrals are evaluated within RecruitIQ to ensure consistent screening, fair consideration, and auditability. The policy clarifies the role of referrals relative to AI recommendations and ensures referrals do not bypass competency-based evaluation.

## Scope
Applies to:

- Employee referral submissions
- Recruiters and hiring managers reviewing referred candidates
- RecruitIQ AI recommendations (match scores, skill gaps, suggested interviews)

This policy is jurisdiction-agnostic.

## Main Content
### 1. What constitutes a valid referral
A valid referral should include:

- Candidate identity and a trustworthy channel to reach them
- The role applied for (or target team)
- High-level context about the candidate’s work scope

Recruiters may request additional information to complete intake.

### 2. Referral does not change evaluation standards
Referred candidates must meet the same evaluation expectations as candidates from any channel.

- AI-assisted screening still applies (skills overlap, experience fit, evidence quality)
- Interview loops follow the same competency model
- Any pass/fail thresholds must be consistent across candidates

### 3. How RecruitIQ AI may be used for referred candidates
Allowed AI usage:

- Provide a match score and rationale based on the competency model
- Identify likely skill gaps to prepare interview questions
- Suggest resume improvements to clarify evidence

Required human review:

- Recruiters must confirm the referral context aligns with the job competencies
- If the referral notes conflict with resume evidence, the panel must resolve based on interviews and documented artifacts

### 4. Fairness and bias mitigation
Recruiters should treat referral context as a starting point, not proof.

- Do not adjust scoring solely due to referral source
- Avoid discounting candidates because of incomplete resume artifacts; instead, probe during structured interviews

### 5. Documentation and audit trail
For each referred candidate, the hiring record should include:

- Referral note summary
- RecruitIQ match score interpretation
- Competency-based screening rationale
- Interview evidence and final decision log

### 6. Communication standards
Recruiters should communicate consistently:

- Acknowledge referral receipt
- Provide status updates aligned with the overall recruiting timeline

### 7. Privacy and data handling (operational)
Referral data should be stored only in approved candidate channels.

- Share referral notes only with relevant hiring stakeholders
- Limit AI prompts to candidate-appropriate content

## Best Practices
- Provide interviewers with a short “referral context brief” that is competency-focused.
- Use AI to identify targeted questions, not to justify a decision.

## Examples
### Example: Referral indicates “systems design strength”
- AI match score is medium.
- Interviewer uses AI skill-gap guidance to ask for concrete design trade-offs.
- Panel decision is based on evidence from the interview and rubric.

## Summary
RecruitIQ treats referrals as an input to candidate intake, not a bypass of competency standards. AI assists with screening and interview preparation, while humans remain responsible for fair, documented evaluation.
