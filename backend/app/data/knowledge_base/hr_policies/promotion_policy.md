# RecruitIQ Promotion Policy (Evidence-Based Growth)

## Purpose
Provide a structured, transparent approach to employee promotions. This policy defines the use of evidence, competency alignment, and AI-supported summarization where applicable. RecruitIQ ensures promotion decisions remain human-led.

## Scope
Applies to:

- Internal candidates for level progression in engineering, data, product, and operations
- People managers, HR partners, and review panels
- RecruitIQ AI features that compile performance evidence

Jurisdiction-neutral guidance.

## Main Content
### 1. Promotion principles
Promotions should be grounded in:

- demonstrated impact in the current scope
- consistent competency performance
- alignment to the target level expectations

RecruitIQ does not use AI to automatically approve promotions.

### 2. Promotion criteria by competency
For each level, a competency model defines expectations:

- technical depth or domain expertise
- execution quality and ownership
- collaboration and stakeholder management
- mentoring and knowledge sharing (where relevant)

Operational rule:

- Candidates must show evidence across multiple quarters or projects.

### 3. Role of AI in promotion preparation
RecruitIQ may support:

- summarizing performance review notes
- extracting recurring evidence themes
- proposing development areas relative to the target competency model

Human control requirements:

- AI summaries must be reviewed for accuracy
- AI must not introduce claims not present in documented performance records

### 4. Review workflow
Recommended steps:

- Manager submits a promotion packet including:
  - target-level justification
  - evidence excerpts (metrics, project outcomes, peer feedback)
  - competency mapping
- Panel reviews independently using rubric definitions
- The panel conducts calibration and decides using evidence

### 5. Documentation expectations
A promotion packet should include:

- competency-by-competency evidence bullets
- impact statement with measurable outcomes where available
- collaboration examples and leadership behaviors
- risk notes (if any) with mitigation and trajectory plan

### 6. Fairness and bias mitigation
To reduce bias:

- require consistent rubric mapping across candidates
- use multiple feedback sources
- ensure promotion timing considerations are documented

### 7. Outcome categories
Typical outcomes:

- Promotion approved
- Promotion deferred with development plan
- Decision not approved with feedback and growth targets

In deferred cases:

- specify milestones and reassessment timeline

## Best Practices
- Use “evidence themes” to structure promotion discussions.
- Maintain a written development plan with measurable expectations.

## Examples
### Example: Deferred promotion due to incomplete leadership evidence
- Evidence: strong execution, moderate stakeholder influence.
- Plan: assign cross-team ownership and mentoring responsibilities.
- Reassessment: after next measurable project milestone.

## Summary
RecruitIQ promotes employees using a competency model and evidence-based review. AI may summarize and assist preparation, but decisions remain human-reviewed, documented, and fair.
