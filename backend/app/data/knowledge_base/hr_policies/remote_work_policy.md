# RecruitIQ Remote Work Policy (Operating Model)

## Purpose
Define expectations for remote work arrangements so teams maintain productivity, inclusion, and accountability. RecruitIQ may use AI tools to help manage scheduling, document summarization, and knowledge discovery, but remote-work decisions remain human-led.

## Scope
Applies to:

- All employees with remote or hybrid schedules
- Managers approving remote arrangements
- HR partners overseeing policy alignment

This document is jurisdiction-agnostic.

## Main Content
### 1. Principles
RecruitIQ remote work expectations should support:

- Equal access to information and opportunities
- Predictable communication rhythms
- Measurable outcomes and transparent planning
- Responsible and secure collaboration

### 2. Eligibility and approval
Remote arrangements are evaluated based on:

- role requirements and collaboration intensity
- ability to meet service levels and deadlines
- onboarding readiness and team integration plan

Approvals:

- Manager proposes arrangement
- HR confirms policy fit and documentation standards

### 3. Communication and availability
Teams should standardize:

- core collaboration hours (time-zone aware)
- meeting norms (agenda, notes, action items)
- response-time expectations

Recommended minimum cadence:

- weekly team sync
- role-specific 1:1s
- asynchronous updates for project status

### 4. Performance management for remote roles
Performance is measured using:

- outcomes (deliverables, quality, reliability)
- collaboration behaviors (knowledge sharing, responsiveness)
- continuous improvement (learning plan, adoption of feedback)

AI support is allowed for:

- consolidating meeting notes
- drafting status summaries

Human control:

- Do not use AI output to change performance ratings without reviewing the underlying evidence.

### 5. Security, privacy, and data handling
Remote work requires:

- secure device practices and access controls
- adherence to approved tooling for sensitive information

AI systems must be used with:

- least-privilege prompts and content minimization
- no sharing of confidential information in unapproved channels

### 6. Inclusion and fairness
RecruitIQ requires:

- equitable meeting participation across locations
- rotation of facilitation and documentation roles
- accessibility best practices for recordings and written summaries

### 7. Onboarding for remote hires
Onboarding must include:

- a structured first-week plan
- mentorship assignment and office-hours
- early access to codebase, processes, and training materials

If RecruitIQ generates onboarding plans:

- the plan is a draft and must be reviewed by the manager
- the employee’s feedback should be incorporated before finalization

## Best Practices
- Use documented “working agreements” per team.
- Maintain a single source of truth (roadmaps, decision logs, meeting notes).

## Examples
### Example: Time-zone overlap challenge
- Team defines overlapping hours for high-synchrony work.
- Weekly asynchronous status reports ensure continuity.

## Summary
RecruitIQ’s remote work policy emphasizes communication predictability, measurable outcomes, security, and inclusion. AI may assist with summarization and planning, but human managers retain decision authority.
