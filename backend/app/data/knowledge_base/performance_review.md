# RecruitIQ Performance Review (Evidence-Based)

## Purpose
Define a performance review framework that uses evidence and competency rubrics. It also describes AI-supported summarization for review packets as decision support only.

## Scope
Applies to:

- All employees participating in performance reviews
- Managers, HR partners, and review panels

Jurisdiction-agnostic.

## Main Content
### 1. Goals of performance reviews
Performance reviews should:

- provide clear feedback and coaching
- align employee outcomes with team and role goals
- support fair, consistent evaluation

### 2. Competency rubric structure
A competency rubric typically includes:

- technical competency (role-specific)
- execution and ownership
- collaboration and communication
- reliability and operational practices

Each rubric entry should define:

- expected behaviors
- evidence examples
- rating anchors (strong, meets, developing)

### 3. Evidence collection
Managers must gather evidence before review discussions:

- project outcomes and delivery milestones
- peer/stakeholder feedback
- quality and reliability indicators
- examples of problem-solving and learning

Avoid relying solely on subjective impressions.

### 4. AI support for performance review packets
RecruitIQ AI may help:

- summarize meeting notes and performance inputs
- highlight evidence themes aligned to the rubric
- draft coaching recommendations based on documented gaps

Human validation requirements:

- verify factual accuracy of any AI-generated summaries
- ensure AI does not add new claims beyond submitted evidence
- treat AI as a draft, not a verdict

### 5. Review meeting guidelines
The review meeting should include:

- a recap of evidence
- discussion of strengths and impact
- improvement areas with specific examples
- agreed next-quarter goals

### 6. Coaching and development plan
A development plan should specify:

- target competencies
- measurable milestones
- feedback sources (mentors, peers)
- reassessment timeline

### 7. Fairness and calibration
To keep reviews comparable:

- calibrate ratings across similar roles
- use rubric anchors consistently
- document rating rationale with evidence

## Best Practices
- Use “evidence bullets” rather than long narratives.
- Keep goals specific and measurable.

## Examples
### Example: Coaching for collaboration
- Evidence: missed stakeholder updates.
- Plan: set a weekly status ritual with action items.

## Summary
RecruitIQ performance reviews are evidence-based and rubric-driven. AI may summarize inputs and draft themes, but human managers validate accuracy and own the final feedback and ratings.
